Graduation thesis. A GINEConv graph neural network that predicts binding affinity (pKd) from a molecule's structure, with atom-level explanations, served through FastAPI and Next.js. Test RMSE 1.56 and Pearson r 0.63 on a PDBBind v2020 subset; the model does not yet use the target protein.
